## Eight stress dimensions

1. ordinary-life landing;
2. ensemble / side-life capacity;
3. relation-specific voice;
4. object thread without plot-mechanism reduction;
5. indirection and ambiguity preservation;
6. culture as action rather than museum display;
7. chapter breath / rhythm / non-aphoristic ending;
8. tragedy without explanatory summary.

These dimensions are downstream literary-design constraints. They are not Evidence.

## Machine meaning of PASS

A P4 machine PASS means only:

- all windows are covered;
- all eight dimensions receive repeated coverage;
- the contract contains sufficient focalizer, side-character, ordinary-action, object, ending and technique diversity;
- each known scenario-specific risk has an explicit countermeasure.

It does **not** mean:

- the scenario is better than another;
- the prose will be good;
- the lost manuscript used this route;
- a chapter may be promoted;
- Chapter 89 blind review is complete.
