The plan's three-layer model becomes a formal permission model:

- Layer 1 → `hard_constraints`
- Layer 2 → `high_probability_generation`
- Layer 3 → `authorial_generation`

A candidate may be admitted from layers 2 or 3 without direct evidence, but it must never contradict layer 1 or be reported as if it came from layer 1.
